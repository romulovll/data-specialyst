## Newsletter Formula

Each issue follows:
1. **TL;DR** — One sentence, what changed and why you care
2. **What Google Announced** — Official version (1 paragraph)
3. **In Plain Language** — Simple translation, no jargon
4. **Real Example** — Before vs After code, copy-paste ready
5. **Why This Matters** — Impact per role (architect, engineer, cert prep)
6. **Try It Yourself** — 5-minute hands-on exercise
7. **One-Liner Takeaway** — The sentence they'll remember
