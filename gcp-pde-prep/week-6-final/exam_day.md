## Exam Day Strategy

### Before the exam
- Light breakfast, water, no heavy studying
- Arrive 15 minutes early (or log in early for online)
- Take a breath. You've been doing this job for 7+ years. You know this.

### During the exam
1. **First pass (90 minutes)**: Answer all questions you're confident about. Flag uncertain ones.
2. **Second pass (30 minutes)**: Return to flagged questions with fresh eyes.
3. **Never leave blanks**: No penalty for wrong answers — always guess.

### Question-reading protocol
1. Read the LAST sentence first (the actual question)
2. Identify the qualifier: "most cost-effective" / "minimum latency" / "least operational overhead"
3. Read the scenario
4. Eliminate wrong answers
5. Pick the BEST answer (not just a correct one)

### Common traps
- **"Serverless" bias**: When the question asks for "minimum operational overhead," the serverless option is usually correct
- **"Existing code" signal**: When they mention "existing Spark/Hadoop jobs" → Dataproc, not Dataflow
- **"Global" signal**: Global + relational → Spanner; Global + NoSQL → Bigtable with replication
- **Two correct answers**: Both might work, but one is MORE correct given the specific constraint. Re-read the qualifier.
